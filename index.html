📍 Vacation Highlights

Watkins Glen State Park

This iconic 1.5-mile path takes you past 19 waterfalls, hidden stone tunnels, and 200-foot cliffs. You will climb 832 stone steps—including walking directly behind Rainbow Falls and Cavern Cascade.

Vineyards

Discover the charm of Finger Lakes Wine Country, where scenic vineyards, breathtaking lake views, and award-winning wineries come together to create an unforgettable escape filled with exceptional wines and local hospitality.

Taughannock Falls State Park

Experience a spectacular stone amphitheater and 215-foot vertical waterfall at the end of the wide and wooded ¾-mile gorge trail. The trail is relatively flat, making it a very accessible and beautiful hike for all ages.

📸 Memory Making

The Finger Lakes are perfect for family-friendly lake adventures! With 11 beautiful glacial lakes, families can enjoy everything from swimming and boating to fishing, paddleboarding, and relaxing beach days.
